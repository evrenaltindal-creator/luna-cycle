## Long description

### Track your cycle

Luna Cycle helps you record period dates and understand your cycle rhythm with a calm calendar and clear summaries. Add start and end dates, review previous cycles and keep your basic cycle view on your device.

### Daily Check-In

Record flow, spotting, cramps, energy, moods, symptoms and personal notes in a daily check-in. You can edit or delete your records. Core tracking remains available in the Free tier.

### Estimates and insights

Luna Cycle may provide approximate cycle information and informational estimates based on the records you enter. Ovulation and fertile-window information is not a definitive medical result. Do not use these outputs for contraception, pregnancy prevention, diagnosis, treatment or emergency medical decisions.

### Reminders

On supported devices, you can schedule local reminders. A private-notification preference can keep reminder wording more general.

### Privacy-first by design

Your cycle records are designed to stay on your device in the current release. Luna Cycle does not operate a remote health-data account or backend for these records. Health records are not used to target ads, sent as advertising request parameters or shared with billing systems.

User-initiated JSON export and import controls help you manage your local records. You control where an exported file is stored, shared or deleted.

### Luna Plus

Luna Plus may be offered as an auto-renewing monthly or yearly store subscription. The premium experience may include an ad-free experience and advanced personal insights. Product name, duration and price are displayed from localized store product details.

The Free tier may show contextual, non-personalized-first banner advertising only in approved low-sensitivity footer placements. Health data is not used to target ads. When Luna Plus is active, ad requests should stop. Renewal and cancellation are managed through the relevant Google Play or Apple App Store account.

### Important health notice

Luna Cycle is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Estimates are approximate and informational. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about medical concerns. In an emergency, use your local emergency services.
