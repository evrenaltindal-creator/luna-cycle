## Long description

### Track your cycle

Luna Cycle helps you record period dates and understand your cycle rhythm with a calm calendar and clear summaries. Add start and end dates, review previous cycles and keep your basic cycle view on your device.

### Daily Check-In

Record flow, spotting, cramps, energy, moods, symptoms and personal notes in a daily check-in. You can edit or delete your records. All current features are free.

### Estimates and insights

After at least three completed cycle intervals are recorded, Luna Cycle may show broad estimated period-start ranges alongside personal insights. It does not present a personal date after only one or two cycles. It does not calculate ovulation or identify fertile or safe days; unmarked calendar days must not be treated as safe. Do not use this app for contraception, pregnancy planning, diagnosis, treatment or emergency medical decisions.

### Reminders

On supported devices, you can schedule local reminders. A private-notification preference can keep reminder wording more general.

### Privacy-first by design

Your cycle records are designed to stay on your device in the current release. Luna Cycle does not operate a remote health-data account or backend for these records.

User-initiated JSON export and import controls help you manage your local records. You control where an exported file is stored, shared or deleted.

### Free first release

All current features are free in this release. There is no in-app purchase, subscription, premium gate or in-app advertising display.

### Important health notice

Luna Cycle is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Estimates are approximate and informational. Consult a qualified healthcare professional about medical concerns. In an emergency, use your local emergency services.
