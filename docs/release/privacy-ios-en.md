# Luna Cycle — iOS Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Service provider: Evren Altındal (Türkiye). Contact: info@ewocom.com.

## Your records and use

Luna Cycle helps you track period start and end dates, flow or spotting, symptoms, mood, energy, and personal notes. These records, app preferences, and the version and time of your Terms of Use acceptance are stored on your device. You do not need to create a Luna account.

Luna Cycle does not upload these health records to a Luna server. The iOS release has no advertising, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or advertising targeting. The app does not sell or send your health records to advertisers.

## Device functions and permissions

Notification permission may be requested for local reminders. The optional app lock uses your iPhone's biometric or device-passcode authentication; Luna Cycle does not receive your fingerprint, face image, or device passcode. You can change permissions in iPhone Settings. Records may be subject to operating-system storage and backup features; manage iCloud and device backups through your own Apple account.

## Backup and sharing

You may export records to a JSON file or import a backup. You choose the storage location and recipient. Files outside the app are subject to the privacy practices of the service you select. Luna Cycle cannot remotely delete those copies. Check the file and recipient before sharing health information.

## Retention and deletion

You can edit or individually delete records, or use “Clear All Data” in Settings. This also removes your Terms of Use acceptance record. Uninstalling the app does not delete backup files stored elsewhere.

## Support and your rights

We process information you email to info@ewocom.com to answer your request. We retain it for as long as needed to respond and meet any applicable legal obligations. You do not need to include health records or backup files in support messages. Send privacy requests to the same address; you may contact competent authorities under applicable law. This policy does not limit mandatory consumer or personal-data rights.

## Security and changes

Protect your device, Apple account, and exported files. No device or software can guarantee absolute security. If product behavior changes, we update this policy and the related store disclosures; material changes will be announced in the app or on the support page.

Luna Cycle is not a medical device. It does not calculate ovulation timing or fertile or safe days and must not be used for contraception, pregnancy planning, diagnosis, or treatment.
